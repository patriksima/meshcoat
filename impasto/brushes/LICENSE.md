The local bundle metadata says `CC-BY-SA` without a version. The author's
release post links the CC BY-SA 4.0 deed and explicitly distinguishes the
brush resources from paintings made using them.

These PNG tips and the images embedded in `impasto-impasto.json` are adaptations:
selected RGBA frames from the A, H and J GIH resources were extracted,
converted to luminance-derived relief proxies, softened, padded and rotated.
Alpha coverage was retained separately. Adaptations are distributed under
the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license. They are not original physical height maps and
do not reproduce Krita's full color-smudge engine or its rotating-light animation.
No endorsement by Emil Maselli is implied.

When redistributing the converted brush resources, retain attribution,
this description of changes and the license link; keep adaptations under
the same license. Commercial use is allowed. The author states that paintings
and brush strokes made with the brushes are yours to decide over; the
resource license does not require artwork or games created with these brushes to be released under CC BY-SA.
